Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers Vice Clips Uploader, a private tool that the owner of the Vice Clips YouTube channels uses to manage those channels.

What the tool accesses

  • Through YouTube API Services, it accesses only the owner's own Vice Clips channels. It uploads and schedules videos, edits their titles and descriptions, and reads channel and video statistics.
  • It does not access any other YouTube user's account or data. It does not collect personal data from viewers.

Storage

OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold or shared with anyone, and are not used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the tool's access at any time on the Google security settings page. Data stored by the tool is deleted within 30 days of revocation, or on request.

TikTok

The same tool also manages the owner's Vice Clips TikTok accounts (@viceclips_tr, @en_viceclips) through TikTok's Login Kit, Display API and Content Posting API. With the owner's permission it reads the account's basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar, follower and like counts) and the list and statistics of the account's own videos, and uploads clips the owner has approved to those accounts. It does not access any other TikTok user's data. TikTok tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never sold or shared. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; stored TikTok data is then deleted within 30 days, or on request.
