Stream highlights, clipped while you sleep.

Vice Clips watches long gaming live streams, finds the moments chat goes wild about, and turns them into captioned vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels, in English, German and Turkish.

How it works

From a six-hour stream to a 60-second Short

The pipeline runs around the clock on a single machine. You only make the final call.

1

Watch

Follows the streamers you choose on Twitch, Kick and YouTube and picks up their finished streams.

2

Score

Reads chat activity, audio peaks and the transcript to rank every moment. Only the strongest few per hour make it through.

3

Render

Cuts a 9:16 clip with facecam on top, burned-in subtitles, a short hook and a caption written for each language.

4

Approve & post

Swipe through drafts, approve the good ones, and they are scheduled to Shorts, TikTok and Reels on a daily plan.

The panel

One dashboard for every clip, channel and language

The web panel is where the work happens. It runs on your own machine, works on desktop and phone, and can be installed as an app.

Home

Everything that needs you, on one screen

Open the panel and see what is waiting, what is going out next and how the channels are doing.

  • Approval queue per language, one tap to start reviewing
  • Followers and views across YouTube, Instagram and TikTok
  • Upcoming posts with time, platform and caption
  • Which followed streamers are live right now
Flow

Review clips like a feed

Drafts play full screen, sorted by score. Approve, skip or reject in a tap, and the next one is already loaded.

  • Switch between English, German and Turkish queues
  • Fix the caption or ask for a re-edit without leaving the clip
  • Rejections with a reason feed back into the filters
  • Approval also works from Telegram when you are away

Built-in guardrails

Bad clips never reach your channel

Every draft passes automatic checks before you see it, and nothing is posted without your approval.

🔇

Muted or silent audio

Clips with platform-muted stretches or missing speech are skipped.

🎵

Copyrighted music

Long music passages, like in-game radio, are detected and dropped.

🚫

Unsafe content

Moments that chat or the transcript flag as explicit are filtered out.

🎮

Right game only

Only the game you follow is clipped, not the rest of the stream.

🗓️

Sensible schedule

Posts are spaced out per platform and language, so channels never spam.

🎥

Credit, always

Every clip names the streamer and links to their channel.

Vice Clips Uploader today

Right now the uploader is the channel owner's private tool. It uploads and schedules approved clips to the owner's own Vice Clips channels and reads those channels' statistics. It does not offer any service to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
